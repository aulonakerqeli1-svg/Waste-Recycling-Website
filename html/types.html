Waste Types

Discover the different types of waste and learn how to recycle them effectively.

Plastic

Plastic waste includes bottles, packaging, and containers. Always separate plastics for recycling.

Paper

Paper and cardboard can be recycled easily. Keep them clean and dry before disposal.

Organic

Organic waste such as food scraps and garden waste can be composted at home or in community bins.

Hazardous

Hazardous waste includes chemicals, batteries, and electronics. Dispose safely.

Metal

Metal cans, foil, and scrap can be recycled. Separate metals from other waste.

Electronic

E-waste includes old electronics. Take them to specialized recycling centers.
